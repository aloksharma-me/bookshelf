# 6 – The Keyboard Derby

>[!AI Summary]
>The development of the iPhone keyboard was a testament to Apple’s focus on innovation through iteration and collaboration. The team faced the challenge of designing a functional touchscreen keyboard, a key component of the iPhone's large touchscreen and minimal-button concept. Early prototypes explored oversized keys, non-QWERTY layouts, and various gestures like swiping and long presses. However, these ideas proved impractical, as they were difficult to use and deviated from familiar typing habits.
>
>The turning point came with the concept of a big-key QWERTY keyboard that displayed multiple letters per key and relied on software to predict words based on the keys tapped. This approach allowed users to type as they would on a traditional keyboard, simplifying the interaction by eliminating gestures and ensuring accuracy through predictive text. Iterative demos played a crucial role in refining these ideas. Each prototype was tested, providing direct feedback that informed the next iteration, enabling rapid problem-solving and improvement.
>
>Apple’s hands-on approach to brainstorming and decision-making, where ideas had to be demonstrated rather than merely discussed, ensured that creativity remained grounded in practicality. The result was an intuitive keyboard experience that became central to the iPhone’s usability and success, highlighting the power of focused collaboration and iterative design.
