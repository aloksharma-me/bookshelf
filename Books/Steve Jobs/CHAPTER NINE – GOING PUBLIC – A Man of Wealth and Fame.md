# Steve Jobs

### CHAPTER NINE – GOING PUBLIC – A Man of Wealth and Fame

#### Options
- When Mike Markkula joined Jobs and Wozniak to turn their fledgling partnership into the Apple Computer Co. in January 1977, they valued it at $5,309.
- By the end of December 1980, Apple would be valued at $1.79 billion.
- Apple went public the morning of December 12, 1980.
- At age twenty-five, he was now worth $256 million.

#### Baby You’re a Rich Man
- Inc. became the first magazine to put him on its cover, in October 1981.
- Time followed in February 1982 with a package on young entrepreneurs.
