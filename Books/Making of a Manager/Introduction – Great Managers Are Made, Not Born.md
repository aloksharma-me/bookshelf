# Introduction – Great Managers Are Made, Not Born
- All that I knew of management could be neatly summarized into two words: meetings and PROMOTION.
- Good design at its core is about understanding people and their needs in order to create the best possible tools for them.
- I’m drawn to design for a lot of the same reasons that I’m drawn to management—it feels like a deeply human endeavor to empower others.
- Great managers are made, not born.
