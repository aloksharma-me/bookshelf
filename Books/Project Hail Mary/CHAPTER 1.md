### CHAPTER 1
The protagonist wakes up in a strange room. He doesn't know his own name, or has any memory of the past. Although, he realises he was in a coma. There are two other people in the room – both are dead. There is a robot arms that helped take care of him while he was unconcious.

He starts exploring the space around him – there's a lab nearby. He realises he knows the names of the scientific instruments and deduces that he might be a scientist.

He sets up experiments to figure out the gravity of the place. It's 15ms^2 as compare to 9.8ms^2 on Earth. He's not on Earth.
