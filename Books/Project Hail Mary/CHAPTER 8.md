### CHAPTER 8
- I check the Centrifuge screen. It shows a pitch rate of 20.71° per second, a total separation of 104 meters, and a “Lab Gravity” of 1.00 g.
- After a quick scan, it tells me the cylinder is made of xenon. “What …?”
- That’s the message. “We’re from the 40 Eridani system. And now we’re here at Tau Ceti.” But there’s even more to it than that. They’re also saying “40 Eridani has a Petrova line, just like Tau Ceti.”
- They want to meet.
